Day routes / Hanoi

Pho, lakes & bia hơi

From tai chi by the lake to egg coffee, bún chả and fresh draught beer on a plastic stool - Hanoi on foot.

Day routes 🇻🇳 Vietnam · Hanoi · Best time: Any day (Hoa Lo closes in the afternoon)

Open route in Google Maps ↗ All routes in the app

Stops

  1. 06:00 Hoan Kiem Lake
    In the morning half the city does tai chi along the shore. Maps
  2. 06:45 Phở Bát Đàn
    This is how I got to know Hanoi: pho at 6am on a plastic stool. Bát Đàn is one of the best-known spots. 1.1 km · Walking 15 min Maps
  3. 08:30 Temple of Literature
    Vietnam's first university - still quiet in the morning. 1.6 km · Walking 21 min Maps
  4. 10:15 St Joseph's Cathedral
    Neo-Gothic in the middle of the maze of lanes. 1.8 km · Walking 24 min Maps
  5. 11:00 Café Giảng
    The birthplace of egg coffee - sweet, creamy, a must. 1 km · Walking 14 min Maps
  6. 12:30 Bún chả Hương Liên
    Bún chả just like Obama and Anthony Bourdain had it. 2.2 km · Walking 30 min Maps
  7. 14:00 Hoa Lo Prison
    Heavy, but important for understanding Vietnam. 1.3 km · Walking 17 min Maps
  8. 16:00 Ngoc Son Temple
    Across the red Huc Bridge to the temple in the lake. 1.2 km · Walking 16 min Maps
  9. 18:00 Bia hơi corner (Tạ Hiện)
    Fresh draught bia hơi for very little money - plastic stool included. 0.7 km · Walking 9 min Maps
