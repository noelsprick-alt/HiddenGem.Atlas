Day routes / Hoi An

A day in lantern light

Into the empty old town early, cao lầu for lunch, a break through the midday heat - and lanterns on the river at night. All on foot.

Day routes 🇻🇳 Vietnam · Hoi An · Best time: Best on the 14th day of the lunar month (full-moon lantern festival, see event calendar)

Open route in Google Maps ↗ All routes in the app

Stops

  1. 06:30 Japanese Covered Bridge
    Early in the morning the old town is yours. Maps
  2. 07:30 Bánh Mì Phượng
    One of Vietnam's most famous bánh mì - breakfast to go. 0.6 km · Walking 9 min Maps
  3. 09:00 Tan Ky Old House
    An old merchant's house from the inside: wood, lacquer, family history. 0.4 km · Walking 5 min Maps
  4. 10:00 Fujian Assembly Hall
    The most colourful assembly hall in the old town. 0.1 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  5. 11:00 Reaching Out Teahouse
    A silent teahouse run by people with hearing and speech impairments - you order with little cards. 0.2 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  6. 12:30 Central Market
    Cao lầu at the food stalls - this noodle dish only exists in Hoi An. Siesta afterwards. 0.3 km · Walking 3 min Maps
  7. 17:00 An Hội night market
    Across the bridge to the islet: street food and lantern stalls. 0.4 km · Walking 5 min Maps
  8. 18:30 Lantern boat on the Thu Bồn
    Take a boat and float a candle on the water. Kitschy? Yes. Beautiful? Also yes. 0.2 km · Walking 3 min Maps
