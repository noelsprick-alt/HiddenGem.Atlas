Day routes / Tirana

Bunkers, bazaar & Blloku

History underground, lunch at the bazaar, views from the Pyramid and aperitivo in the once-forbidden quarter - all on foot.

Day routes 🇦🇱 Albania · Tirana · Best time: Any day (Bunk'Art may be closed on some Mondays - check first)

Open route in Google Maps ↗ All routes in the app

Stops

  1. 09:00 Skanderbeg Square
    The huge central square - almost empty in the morning. Maps
  2. 09:30 Et'hem Bey Mosque
    A small mosque with beautiful frescoes. 0.1 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  3. 10:15 Bunk'Art 2
    A nuclear bunker in the city centre. My favourite is Bunk'Art 1 on the outskirts - if you have time, take the bus. 0.2 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  4. 12:15 New Bazaar (Pazari i Ri)
    Lunch among the market stalls - Albanian food at fair prices. 0.7 km · Walking 9 min Maps
  5. 14:00 Pyramid of Tirana
    Once the Hoxha museum, now a colourful hangout - you can climb it. 1.1 km · Walking 15 min Maps
  6. 15:00 Grand Park & Lake
    A walk by the lake - Tirana's green lung. 1.1 km · Walking 14 min Maps
  7. 17:30 Blloku
    Once the sealed-off quarter of the party elite, now cafés, bars and aperitivo. 0.8 km · Walking 10 min Maps
