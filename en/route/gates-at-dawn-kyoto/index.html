Day routes / Kyoto

Gates at dawn

From an empty Fushimi Inari through Higashiyama and Gion to the lanterns of the Shirakawa canal. One short train ride, otherwise on foot.

Day routes 🇯🇵 Japan · Kyoto · Best time: Weekdays (much busier at weekends)

Open route in Google Maps ↗ All routes in the app

Stops

  1. 06:00 Fushimi Inari Taisha
    My moment: at 6am the thousand gates are almost all yours. Maps
  2. 09:00 Kiyomizu-dera
    A wooden terrace above the forest - still relaxed in the morning. Maps
  3. 10:30 Sannenzaka & Ninenzaka
    Old lanes with teahouses - grab a coffee at % Arabica by the Yasaka pagoda. 0.7 km · Walking 9 min Maps
  4. 11:30 Yasaka Shrine & Maruyama Park
    The shrine at the end of Gion, with the park behind it. 0.8 km · Walking 11 min Maps
  5. 12:15 Hanamikoji (Gion)
    Wooden façades and teahouses. Please don't chase maiko for photos - private lanes have a photo ban. 0.4 km · Walking 6 min Maps
  6. 13:15 Nishiki Market
    "Kyoto's kitchen": lunch from stall to stall. 1.2 km · Walking 17 min Maps
  7. 15:00 Kamo River banks
    A break on the riverbank - like the locals. 0.8 km · Walking 11 min Maps
  8. 17:30 Pontocho
    A narrow alley full of restaurants, with river terraces in summer. 0.3 km · Walking 4 min Maps
  9. 20:00 Gion Shirakawa
    To finish, the canal in lantern light - Kyoto at its quietest. 0.5 km · Walking 7 min Maps
