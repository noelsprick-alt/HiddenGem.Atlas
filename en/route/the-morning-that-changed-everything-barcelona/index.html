Day routes / Barcelona

The morning that changed everything

My most formative trip as a day route: start at 7am at the harbour, then old town, El Born, beach and cava - all on foot.

Day routes 🇪🇸 Spain · Barcelona · Best time: Mon-Sat (La Boqueria is closed on Sundays)

Open route in Google Maps ↗ All routes in the app

Stops

  1. 07:00 Port Vell
    My moment: writing postcards while the city is still asleep. Maps
  2. 08:15 Mercat de la Boqueria
    Breakfast at a counter inside the market - no crowds yet in the morning. 1.5 km · Walking 20 min Maps
  3. 09:45 Cathedral & Gothic Quarter
    Through the lanes of the Barri Gòtic to the cathedral. 0.6 km · Walking 8 min Maps
  4. 11:00 Santa Maria del Mar
    The most beautiful church in town - simple, soaring, full of light. 0.6 km · Walking 8 min Maps
  5. 12:30 El Xampanyet
    Tapas and cava standing at the bar - an institution in El Born. 0.1 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  6. 14:15 Parc de la Ciutadella
    Siesta in the park, quick detour to the Arc de Triomf. 0.8 km · Walking 11 min Maps
  7. 16:30 Barceloneta beach
    Toes in the sand, soak up the sun. 1.5 km · Walking 20 min Maps
  8. 19:30 Can Paixano (La Xampanyeria)
    Cava and bocadillos at the counter - loud, packed, glorious. 1 km · Walking 13 min Maps
  9. 21:30 Paradiso
    A speakeasy behind a fridge door - one of the best-known cocktail bars in the world. 0.5 km · Walking 7 min Maps
