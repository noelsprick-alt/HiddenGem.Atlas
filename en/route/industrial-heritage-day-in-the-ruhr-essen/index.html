Day routes / Essen

Industrial heritage day in the Ruhr

World Heritage in the morning, the coking plant in the afternoon, art and the "Rü" in the evening. One tram ride, otherwise on foot.

Day routes 🇩🇪 Germany · Essen · Best time: Tue-Sun (Museum Folkwang is closed on Mondays)

Open route in Google Maps ↗ All routes in the app

Stops

  1. 10:00 Zollverein Colliery (Shaft XII)
    The double headframe - the symbol of the Ruhr area. Maps
  2. 10:30 Ruhr Museum
    Up the orange escalator into the old coal washery. 0.2 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  3. 12:45 Casino Zollverein
    Lunch in the former compressor hall. 0.1 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  4. 14:15 Zollverein Coking Plant
    The cooler sister of the colliery - with a pool in summer. 0.8 km · Walking 11 min Maps
  5. 16:15 Museum Folkwang
    Tram 107 into town. The permanent collection is free. Maps
  6. 18:00 Rüttenscheider Straße
    Dinner and drinks on the "Rü". 1.7 km · Walking 22 min Maps
