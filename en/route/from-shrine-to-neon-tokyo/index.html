Day routes / Tokyo

From shrine to neon

A calm start in the Meiji Shrine forest, pop culture in Harajuku and Shibuya at night - entirely on foot.

Day routes 🇯🇵 Japan · Tokyo · Best time: Any day; book the Shibuya Sky ticket in advance

Stops

  1. 09:00 Meiji Shrine
    Through the giant wooden gate into a forest in the middle of the city. Maps
  2. 10:30 Takeshita Street
    Harajuku pop culture, crêpes and wild shops. 0.8 km · Walking 11 min Maps
  3. 11:30 Cat Street
    A calmer side street with vintage and design shops. 0.6 km · Walking 8 min Maps
  4. 12:30 Harajuku Gyoza Rou
    Almost nothing but gyoza on the menu - that's why it's good. Expect a queue at lunch. 0.1 km · Walking 2 min Maps
  5. 14:00 Yoyogi Park
    Picnics, musicians, dancers - Tokyo's living room. 1.5 km · Walking 20 min Maps
  6. 16:30 Shibuya Sky
    Up to the rooftop above the crossing for sunset. 2.1 km · Walking 28 min Maps
  7. 18:30 Shibuya Crossing
    My moment: neon, people, energy - Tokyo in its purest form. 0.3 km · Walking 3 min Maps
  8. 19:30 Nonbei Yokocho
    Tiny bars with a handful of seats in an alley by the tracks. 0.2 km · Walking 3 min Maps
