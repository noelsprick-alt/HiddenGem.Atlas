Berlin: history on every corner

In hardly any other city do you so often stand where world history happened - and an hour later in a bar on a car-park roof. I have been to Berlin, and exactly this mix is what the city is about.

What makes Berlin special

Berlin is unfinished - and wants to stay that way. Old airports become parks, listening stations become graffiti galleries, and on Sundays strangers sing karaoke in Mauerpark in front of hundreds of people. And supposedly Berlin has more bridges than Venice.

A little history

In 1948/49 the Allies supplied West Berlin via the airlift - the "raisin bombers" landed at Tempelhof. In 1961 the Wall was built, around 155 kilometres long, and for 28 years the city was divided. On 9 November 1989 it fell. Currywurst is a Berlin invention too, by the way: Herta Heuwer first sold it in 1949.

My tip

Mauerpark on a Sunday - the flea market and karaoke in the amphitheatre are Berlin in its purest form. Add street food in Markthalle Neun and Friedrichshain around the RAW site in the evening. And cycle down the runway of Tempelhofer Feld once.

Crazy places

Teufelsberg - a hill of war rubble with an abandoned US listening station on top. Spreepark, the GDR's amusement park whose operator fled to Peru. And a pool floating in the Spree in an old barge hull. All on my map.
