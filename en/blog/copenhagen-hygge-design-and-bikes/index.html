Copenhagen: hygge, design and bikes

Copenhagen is the city where you swim in the harbour, ski on a waste-to-energy plant and get everywhere by bike. I have been there - and Copenhagen shows how relaxed a big city can be.

What makes Denmark special

Hygge - the Danish word for cosiness - is a way of life here: candles, wood, plenty of time. Add to that design on every corner and a city with more bikes than cars. The harbour water is so clean that there are bathing spots right in the city centre.

A little history

Bishop Absalon founded the city as a fortress in the 12th century. The colourful Nyhavn harbour was built in the 17th century, and Hans Christian Andersen lived in several of its houses. In 1971 activists occupied an old military site and proclaimed the Freetown of Christiania - it still exists today.

My tip

Rent a bike and let yourself drift - through Christianshavn, up the spiral tower of the Church of Our Saviour and street food at Reffen in the evening. If you are brave, jump into the Islands Brygge harbour bath. And north of the city lies Bakken, the oldest operating amusement park in the world - free entry.
