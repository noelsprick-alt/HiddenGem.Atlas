Barcelona: the morning that changed everything

There are trips you take - and trips that do something to you. Barcelona 2023 was the second kind for me.

My experience

At 7 am I sat alone at Port Vell writing postcards while my friends were already at the airport. For me Barcelona is the trip that changed everything - because of one single, quiet morning.

What makes Barcelona special

Beach and big city in one, plus Gaudí's buildings that look as if they grew rather than were built. People speak Catalan here, drink vermouth at midday and eat late - hardly any restaurant is full before 9 pm.

A little history

The Romans founded the settlement of Barcino here. In the 19th century industry made Barcelona grow fast, and the new Eixample district with its octagonal blocks was built - work on the Sagrada Família has been going on since 1882. For the 1992 Olympic Games the city opened up to the sea: many of today's city beaches were only created then.

My tip

Set your alarm once, go to the harbour before the city wakes up - and write someone a postcard. At sunset climb up to the Bunkers del Carmel, and in the evening try an absinthe bar from 1820 or a speakeasy behind a fridge door. Both are on my map.
