Tunisia: medina, sea and a taste of the Sahara

For many people Tunisia is just a beach holiday. I was in Sousse - and behind the hotel beach waits one of the most beautiful old towns in North Africa.

What makes Tunisia special

Roman ruins that would be world stars in Italy, medinas with white walls and blue doors and a desert where "Star Wars" film sets still stand. Plus couscous, brik and mint tea with pine nuts.

A little history

Carthage, Rome's great rival, stood here. In antiquity Sousse was called Hadrumetum, and kilometres of early Christian catacombs lie beneath the town. In the 8th century the Ribat was built, a fortress from whose tower you look out over the city. And in December 2010 the Arab Spring began in Tunisia.

My tip

The medina of Sousse is a UNESCO World Heritage Site - go in early, climb the Ribat and look out over the rooftops. A day trip to El Jem is worth it, home to one of the largest Roman amphitheatres in the world.

Crazy but true

Monty Python filmed "Life of Brian" at the Ribat of Monastir. In Matmata you can spend the night in the cave that was Luke Skywalker's home. And in the desert near Tozeur the sand is slowly swallowing the set of Mos Espa. All on my map.
