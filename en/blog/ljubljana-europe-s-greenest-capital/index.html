Ljubljana: Europe's greenest capital

Ljubljana is the greenest capital I know - car-free, everything on foot, cafés by the river. I was there in 2024.

What makes Ljubljana special

The old town has been closed to cars for years, and in 2016 Ljubljana was named European Green Capital. A dragon sits everywhere - the city's coat of arms. Legend has it that Jason and the Argonauts defeated it here.

A little history

The Romans already had a town here called Emona. After a severe earthquake in 1895 large parts of Ljubljana were rebuilt, and many Art Nouveau façades date from that time. The city's face today was shaped above all by one man: the architect Jože Plečnik - the Triple Bridge and the market colonnades are his.

My experience and my tip

My advice from experience: the central market in the morning, sitting by the river at midday and Metelkova in the evening, the brightly painted former barracks full of bars. On Fridays from spring to autumn restaurants from all over the country cook at the market - the "Open Kitchen". And for the view from above: the café in Nebotičnik, one of Europe's first high-rises.
