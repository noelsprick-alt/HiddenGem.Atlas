Why Düsseldorf feels like Japan every May

Once a year the Rhine promenade in Düsseldorf turns into a little Japan: cosplayers, taiko drums, sushi stalls and, at the end, fireworks over the river. In May 2025 I was right in the middle of it - Japan on my doorstep.

What makes Düsseldorf so Japanese

Düsseldorf has one of the largest Japanese communities in Europe. Around Immermannstraße - "Little Tokyo" - there are Japanese supermarkets, bookshops, bakeries and ramen shops with queues outside at lunchtime. In Niederkassel there is even a Japanese Buddhist temple, the EKŌ House, with a garden and tea house.

A little history

From the 1950s Japanese companies opened their European headquarters on the Rhine - Düsseldorf sat in the middle of an industrial region and close to the ports. With the companies came families, schools and restaurants. Japan Day has been held since 2002, and the Japanese fireworks in the evening draw hundreds of thousands to the riverbank every year.

My experience

I was in Japan in 2022 - and at Japan Day I had exactly that feeling again for a few hours: that mix of order, playfulness and kindness. My plan for you: drift along the Rhine promenade by day, have lunch on Immermannstraße and secure a spot by the water in good time for the fireworks.

Hidden gems around Little Tokyo

My favourite day in Düsseldorf is a completely different one, by the way: Flingern. Saturday market at Hermannplatz, cake at Café Hüftgold and browsing at LANGBRETT in the old petrol station. You will find all these places on my hidden gems map.
