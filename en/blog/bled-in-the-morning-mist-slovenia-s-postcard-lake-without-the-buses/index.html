Bled in the morning mist - Slovenia's postcard lake without the buses

Lake Bled is Slovenia's postcard picture - island, church, castle on the cliff. By day you share it with tour buses. At six in the morning it is all yours.

What makes Slovenia special

More than half of the country is forest, and Slovenia is the only country with "love" in its name. Between the Alps, karst caves and the Adriatic there is often just a two-hour drive. The island in Lake Bled is, by the way, the country's only natural island.

A little history

Bled Castle was first mentioned in the year 1004 and is regarded as Slovenia's oldest castle. The pletna oarsmen who take you to the island are said to owe their privilege to Empress Maria Theresa. And Yugoslav leader Tito had a summer villa built on the shore. In 1991 Slovenia became independent after a ten-day war.

My experience

I was in Slovenia in 2024, and my hidden gem in Bled is not a place but a time of day: the lake in the morning mist. Before the first buses it is silent, just a few anglers and the water. Afterwards I walked up to the Ojstrica viewpoint - and only then had breakfast.

My tip

Combine Bled with Vintgar Gorge (boardwalks over turquoise water) and treat yourself to Bled cream cake - baked to the same recipe since 1953. On my map you will also find the Russian Chapel on Vršič Pass and Predjama Castle, which was built into a cliff.
