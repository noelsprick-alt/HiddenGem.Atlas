Luxembourg: a fortress above the gorge

Luxembourg is often underrated as a country you just drive through. Yet its capital hangs over a gorge like a fortress. I was there in March 2025 – under clouds that looked as if someone had ordered them specially for a black-and-white photo.

Pont Adolphe

The stone arch bridge from 1903 spans the Pétrusse valley. From the edge of the upper town you get the bridge, the gorge and the city's towers in a single frame.

The Grund

Down by the Alzette lies the Grund, the old quarter of the lower town. Colourful houses are reflected in the river while the fortress walls tower above you. It feels like a village in the middle of a capital.

World Heritage you can stroll through

The old town and its fortifications have been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1994. In the evening, when the Grand Ducal Palace is lit up, the lanes go quiet – and the city feels even smaller than it is.

My tip

Since 2020, public transport across Luxembourg has been free – buses, trams and second-class trains. So a day trip works perfectly without a car. Bring comfortable shoes: between the upper and lower town it's uphill and downhill all the time.
