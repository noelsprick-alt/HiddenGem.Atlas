Bratislava: the underrated capital

Most people pass Bratislava by on the way between Vienna and Budapest. I went there - and Bratislava is small, relaxed and wonderfully quirky.

What makes Bratislava special

Bratislava is the only capital in the world that borders two countries: Austria and Hungary. You can walk the old town in an hour - and it is full of bronze figures, above all Čumil, the worker peeking out of a manhole.

A little history

Under the name Pressburg the city was the coronation city of Hungarian kings for almost 300 years - kings and queens were crowned in St Martin's Cathedral from 1563 to 1830, Maria Theresa among them. The communist era brought the concrete housing blocks and the UFO Bridge. Since the peaceful split from Czechia in 1993 Bratislava has been the capital of Slovakia.

My tip

Bratislava combines beautifully with Vienna - the two capitals are only about an hour apart. In the evening go up to the UFO deck on the bridge for sunset. And take a look at the radio building shaped like an upside-down pyramid, a regular on lists of the world's ugliest buildings.
