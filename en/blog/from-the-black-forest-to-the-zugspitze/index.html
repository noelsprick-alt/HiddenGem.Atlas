From the Black Forest to the Zugspitze

Freiburg, the Black Forest, Strasbourg and at the very top the Zugspitze: for me the south means forest, half-timbering and mountains. I have been to all of them - and in a small area you find three worlds here.

What makes the south special

Freiburg is regarded as one of the sunniest cities in Germany, the Black Forest as the home of the cuckoo clock, and with the Zugspitze this is where Germany's highest mountain stands - 2,962 metres. Across the Rhine you are in Alsace in minutes, where people speak French and cook German.

A little history

Cuckoo clocks have been made in the Black Forest since the 18th century. Between 1870 and 1945 Strasbourg changed nationality four times - today the European Parliament meets here. And Freiburg's Bächle, the little water channels in the lanes, date back to the Middle Ages.

My tips

Freiburg: the cathedral market on Saturday, a "Lange Rote" sausage in your hand and up the Schlossberg - and watch out for the Bächle: step into one and you will supposedly marry someone from Freiburg. Black Forest: the Black Forest High Road with a stop at Mummelsee. Zugspitze: up by cog railway, down by cable car and into Partnach Gorge early. Strasbourg: La Petite France in the morning and the rooftop terrace of the Barrage Vauban.

Crazy but true

In Strasbourg Cathedral the apostles parade past an astronomical clock every day at 12:30 and a rooster crows. At the Triberg Waterfalls squirrels eat out of your hand. Both on my map.
