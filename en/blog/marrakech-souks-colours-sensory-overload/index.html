Marrakech: souks, colours, sensory overload

Marrakech is loud, colourful and exhausting – and then you walk through an unassuming door and find yourself in a riad. Silence, a pool in the courtyard, tiles in every colour, plants in big pots. I was there in April 2025, and that switch between chaos and calm is what stays with you.

The riad effect

A dip in the courtyard pool during the day, coffee and a shake on a terrace under palm trees at lunchtime. And in the evening a candlelit courtyard, the pool glowing, conversations getting quieter. Outside the medina is raging – inside, hardly any of it reaches you.

Jemaa el-Fna

Just a few minutes' walk away is Jemaa el-Fna, the city's most famous square. In the evening: food stalls, musicians, storytellers and one big swirl. UNESCO protects the square as a cultural space – not because of a building, but because of the people who fill it every night.

My tip

Stay in a riad, not in a concrete hotel on the outskirts. The contrast between the bustle outside and the calm inside is the real experience. And head into the souks without a plan – getting lost is part of it.
