2. Crazy places

KGB Museum💎 Hidden gem

With the real cells. 4.6 stars on Google. Visitors mention about €6 and 1.5 hours; a moving place.

🕒 Wed–Sun, closed Mon/Tue according to Google

📍 View in Google Maps

Frank Zappa monument & Writers’ Lane💎 Hidden gem

The world’s first Frank Zappa monument (Zappa was never in Lithuania) and Writers’ Lane with hundreds of small artworks on the wall.

📍 View in Google Maps

Hill of Crosses💎 Hidden gem

A bit further away: thousands of crosses. 4.6 stars on Google. Visitors: reachable only by car/tour, open around the clock, no ticket.

What makes Lithuania special

Lithuania is the country where basketball is almost a religion and where you eat kepta duona (fried garlic bread) with beer. According to legend Grand Duke Gediminas dreamed of an iron wolf and then founded the city. In 1989 two million people formed a human chain from Vilnius to Tallinn, and in 1990 Lithuania declared independence as the first Soviet republic.
