Gran Canaria is often called a “continent in miniature” – desert in the morning, pine forest at midday, harbour in the evening. Prices and times are guidelines (2026).

My story in Gran Canaria

I was on the island, and exactly this variety is what sticks. My tip: sunrise in the dunes of Maspalomas, the mountains and Roque Nublo in the afternoon, fish in the harbour of Puerto de Mogán at night.

Getting there and around

Gran Canaria has a large airport in the east; buses link the towns. For mountains and harbour villages a rental car or a tour is practical; roads are winding.

1. Must-sees

Dunes of Maspalomas

Sunrise in the dunes. 4.8 stars on Google from over 22,000 reviews. Visitors advise water and sunscreen and warn of midday heat.

📍 View in Google Maps

Roque Nublo

The rock finger at about 1,800 metres in the centre. 4.7 stars on Google. Visitors report limited parking, bus and hike are a good option.

Puerto de Mogán

Fish in the harbour at night.

2. Hidden gem

Cueva Pintada in Gáldar💎 Hidden gem

A place most beach holidaymakers never see: the painted cave of the ancient Canarians. 4.6 stars on Google. Visitors recommend the guided tour (about €6 according to one guest).

🕒 Tue–Sun about 10 am–6 pm, closed Mondays according to Google

What makes Gran Canaria special

Long before the Spaniards the ancient Canarians lived here. At the end of the 15th century Castile conquered the island. In 1492 Columbus landed in Las Palmas on his first voyage to America.
