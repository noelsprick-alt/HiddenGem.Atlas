My story in Hanoi

This is how I got to know Hanoi: pho at 6 am on a plastic stool – better than any restaurant. My advice: head to Hoan Kiem Lake early while the city does tai chi, and have a bia hơi in the old quarter at night, freshly brewed and for small change. In February 2026 I travelled once from north to south through Vietnam.

What makes Vietnam special

Mopeds everywhere, plastic stools by the roadside serving the country’s best food. Vietnam was under Chinese rule for about a thousand years, later French colonial rule – the baguettes for bánh mì are a legacy. The country was reunified in 1976.
