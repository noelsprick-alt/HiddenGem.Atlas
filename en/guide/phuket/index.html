Phuket is Thailand’s largest island and for many the entry to the country. There are plenty of beaches – but the best moments are often away from them: at the market, in a shrine, in a canoe.

Prices and times are guidelines (2026).

My story in Phuket

Almost three weeks in Thailand in February 2026: first Phuket, then Krabi. I had expected beach. The Big Buddha sits 45 metres high in white marble above the island – from below he looks almost unreal. In Phuket’s old town I stumbled between colourful Sino-Portuguese houses into a Chinese shrine with golden dragons and incense sticks. And at the market there they were: fried crickets and silkworm pupae, paid by the spoonful. Next to them durian, dragon fruit and longan, as if it were all the most normal thing in the world. Getting to Phuket and around

Phuket has an international airport served from Bangkok and other hubs. On the island scooters, taxi apps and songthaews are common; agree taxi prices beforehand. If you ride a scooter you need a valid licence and a helmet – the risk is real, inform yourself beforehand.

1. Must-sees

Big Buddha

45 metres high in white marble above the island. 4.6 stars on Google from over 40,000 reviews. Admission is free according to visitors; dress appropriately (shoulders and knees covered).

🕒 daily about 6 am–6:30 pm according to Google

Tip: Monkeys around the site: keep loose items stowed.

2. Hidden gems & experiences

Phang Nga Bay by canoe💎 Hidden gem

Limestone cliffs, caves and calm water: visitors describe canoe tours through the bay as a highlight. 4.7 stars on Google. Tours start early in the morning and last until late afternoon according to visitors.

Tip: Book early; the good slots go quickly.

What makes Thailand special

Thailand is the only country in Southeast Asia never colonised by a European power – people are proud of that to this day. Over 90 percent are Buddhists, and small spirit houses with offerings stand outside houses and shops everywhere. Until 1939 the country was called Siam.

My verdict: Phuket for the start, Krabi for the landscape. See my Krabi guide.
