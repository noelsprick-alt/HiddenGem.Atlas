My story in Freiburg

My tip: the Saturday Münstermarkt – grab a “Lange Rote” sausage, then climb the Schlossberg. And watch out for the Bächle: legend says whoever steps in will marry someone from Freiburg.

1. Must-sees

Münstermarkt

On Saturday a “Lange Rote” sausage in your hand.

📍 View in Google Maps

Schlossberg

With a view over the Minster and the Black Forest. 4.7 stars on Google. Visitors recommend walking up or taking the Schlossbergbahn; the tower is 35 metres high.

📍 View in Google Maps

2. Hidden gems

Die Bächle💎 Hidden gem

The small water channels in the lanes have existed since the Middle Ages. Whoever steps in will supposedly marry someone from Freiburg.
