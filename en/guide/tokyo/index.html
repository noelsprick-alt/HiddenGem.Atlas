My story in Tokyo

Shibuya at night is Tokyo in its purest form for me: neon, people, energy. What impressed me most in Japan, though, was how people treat each other – kindness costs nothing. My advice: Meiji Shrine and Harajuku by day, Shibuya at sunset.

Getting there and around

Tokyo is served by Narita and Haneda airports; trains and buses take you into the city. The subway network is dense; check current tickets beforehand.

1. Must-sees

Meiji Shrine

A forest walk in the middle of Tokyo. 4.7 stars on Google from over 54,000 reviews. Visitors say: astonishingly quiet, eating and drinking not allowed in the shrine area.

📍 View in Google Maps

Harajuku

Right next to the shrine: fashion, crêpes and street scene.

📍 View in Google Maps

Shibuya Crossing

To Shibuya at sunset: neon, people, energy. 4.5 stars on Google. Visitors recommend watching from above and crossing once yourself.

📍 View in Google Maps

What makes Japan special

Politeness is no cliché here: people queue properly, lost items come back, trains run to the second. At the same time Japan is wonderfully crazy.
