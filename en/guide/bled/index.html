My story in Bled

I was in Slovenia in 2024, and my hidden gem in Bled is not a place but a time of day: the lake in the morning mist. Before the first buses it is quiet, just a few anglers and the water. Then I walked up to the Ojstrica viewpoint – and only then had breakfast.

Getting to Bled

Bled lies about 55 km north-west of Ljubljana. Buses (around an hour) and trains to Lesce-Bled with a bus onward run from Ljubljana; check timetables beforehand. By car it takes about an hour, parking at the lake is scarce in summer. A day trip from Ljubljana is doable, but staying overnight gets you the morning stillness.

1. At the lake & above it

The lake in the morning mist💎 Hidden gem🆓 Free to experience

Before the first tour buses the lake is all yours – just a few anglers and the water. So get up early. The island in Lake Bled is, by the way, the only natural island in Slovenia.

Tip: A walk around the lake takes about 1.5 to 2 hours. The pletna rowers take you to the island; according to legend they got their privilege from Empress Maria Theresa.

Ojstrica viewpoint💎 Hidden gem🆓 Free to experience

After that I walked up to the Ojstrica viewpoint. The climb from the lake takes about 30 minutes and is steep, the last stretch goes over rock.

Tip: Sturdy shoes – slippery after rain. It is emptier early in the morning.

Bled Castle

The castle was already mentioned in 1004 and is considered the oldest in Slovenia. Admission is fairly high (around €28), but the view from the courtyard is the classic postcard view.

🕒 daily about 8 am–6 pm

Tip: Around 10 am it is calmer than at midday.

📍 Open & save in Google Maps

Pletna boat to the island

To the island in Lake Bled you go by “pletna”, a traditional wooden boat rowed by a boatman. On the island stands the Church of the Assumption with the wishing bell tower; whoever rings the bell may make a wish. The trip takes about an hour including a stay (check prices beforehand).

Alternatively you can rent a rowing boat or walk around the lake.

Tip: Go early in the morning and ring the wishing bell.

Walk around the lake🆓 Free to experience

A path leads about six kilometres around the lake and takes about 1.5 to 2 hours. You see castle, island and mountains from changing angles. The path is flat and easy to walk.

Start early in the morning when it is quiet.

Tip: Start early in the morning.

2. Surroundings & food

Vintgar Gorge

Wooden walkways above turquoise water, waterfalls and rock walls. The route is roughly 5–6 km long. My tip: book tickets online in advance for a time slot and allow an extra 15–20 minutes for the shuttle from the car park to the entrance.

📍 Open & save in Google Maps

Bled cream cake

The Bled cream cake (kremšnita) has been baked to the same recipe since 1953. At Café Park by the lake you get it with a lake view – the cake is the reason to sit here.

Tip: Prices at the lakeside café are upscale – one slice is enough for the view.

Russian Chapel at Vršič & Predjama Castle

On my map you will also find the Russian Chapel on the Vršič Pass and Predjama Castle, which was built into a rock wall. Both can be combined as a trip by car.

Lake Bohinj🆓 Free to experience

Lake Bohinj lies about 25 kilometres from Bled in the Triglav National Park and is calmer and more natural than Bled. Here you can swim, hike and kayak. A cable car leads to Vogel with a wide view.

Plan half a day to a whole day. Bus or car possible (check timetables beforehand).

Tip: Plan half a day and swim in the lake.

📍 Open & save in Google Maps

Savica waterfall

The Savica waterfall lies above Lake Bohinj and plunges into a gorge. From a car park steps lead down to the fall (about 500 steps). It is a popular destination for nature lovers (check admission and times beforehand).

Wear sturdy shoes.

Tip: Wear sturdy shoes.

Triglav National Park & hikes🆓 Free to experience

Triglav National Park includes the Julian Alps with hiking trails for every fitness level, from short walks to high-alpine tours. Mount Triglav is Slovenia’s highest peak and national symbol. Find out beforehand about weather and difficulty of the tours.

High-alpine tours only with suitable equipment or a mountain guide.

Tip: Plan tours according to weather and fitness.

What makes Slovenia special

More than half the country is forest, and Slovenia is the only country with “love” in its name. Between Alps, karst caves and Adriatic it is often only two hours’ drive. Yugoslavia’s leader Tito had a summer villa built on the shore at Bled. In 1991 Slovenia became independent after a Ten-Day War.

FAQ

Is one day enough in Bled?

Yes, if you start early: lake, Ojstrica, Vintgar and cake fit into one long day.

When is the lake emptiest?

In the morning before the first tour buses – Noel’s hidden gem is exactly that time of day.

Is Bled doable as a day trip from Ljubljana?

Yes, by bus or train plus bus in about an hour. If you want the morning stillness, stay overnight.
