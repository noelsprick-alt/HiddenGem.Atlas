My story in New York

I was there – and the best part: many of the city’s most impressive experiences cost nothing at all. My tip: walk the Brooklyn Bridge towards Manhattan early in the morning, then along the High Line and lunch at Chelsea Market. In the evening choose Williamsburg over Times Square. And for the Statue of Liberty: the free Staten Island Ferry instead of an expensive boat tour.

Getting there and around

New York is served by JFK, Newark and LaGuardia; as a German citizen you need ESTA authorisation to enter – check current rules beforehand. Within the city the subway is the backbone.

What makes New York special

Before it was New York it was called New Amsterdam. In 1664 the English took over. Through Ellis Island about twelve million immigrants came to the USA between 1892 and 1954.
