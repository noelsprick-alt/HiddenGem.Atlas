My story in Hamburg

I was in Hamburg, Bremen, Lübeck and Rügen – and every corner tells of the time when Europe’s mightiest merchants sat here. My tip for Hamburg: the fish market very early on a Sunday, then the HADAG ferry from the Landungsbrücken – a harbour tour for the price of a bus ticket. In the evening, up to the Elbphilharmonie Plaza.

What makes the north special

A “Moin” works at any time of day. In the Middle Ages the Hanseatic League dominated trade in the North and Baltic Seas. The houses are red brick, the weather changes hourly.
