My story in Bad Gastein & Lungau

Austria is no ordinary holiday country for me: I worked at a hotel reception in Bad Gastein – the travel world from the other side of the counter. A place with a waterfall rushing through its centre, surrounded by Belle Époque hotels that look like from another time. My hidden gem: the waterfall that rushes right through the village. In the evening, when the day-trippers are gone, the Belle Époque backdrop feels almost melancholic.

In August 2026 we went to the Lungau, one of the quietest corners of Salzburg state. The way leads over meadows and ridges, a summit cross stands at the top and below lies the Landawirsee – crystal clear and, let’s say, refreshing. I went in anyway. Completely. Head first. Afterwards there was Kaiserschmarrn at the Landawirseehütte with a cool regional beer. We stayed overnight at the hut. And on the way back a herd of white cows with their calves stood in the middle of the alp and watched us very closely.

Getting there and around

Bad Gastein lies on the Tauern railway and is reachable by train from Salzburg; check current connections beforehand. The Lungau lies in the south of Salzburg state and is best reached by car or bus.

You reach Bad Gastein by train from Salzburg in about 1.5 hours (check timetables beforehand); the station is in the middle of the village. For the Lungau and the hikes a car is practical, but buses also run. In the region there are often guest cards that include bus and rail – ask your accommodation.

Cable cars have fixed operating times and close in bad weather; check the operating notice beforehand.

1. Must-sees

Gastein waterfall

The waterfall in the middle of the village. 4.7 stars on Google from over 3,000 reviews; freely accessible, visitors mention viewpoints along the gorge.

The Gastein waterfall thunders in several stages through the middle of the village – around it stand Belle Époque hotels, cafés and viewpoints. With a total height of more than 300 metres (according to local information) it is one of the most spectacular waterfalls lying directly in a town. There are bridges and paths from which you can see it from below and above.

The spray cools the air noticeably, and in winter ice banners form. A walk around the waterfall takes half an hour to an hour; in the evening some areas are lit. Wear non-slip shoes, as the path can be wet.

Tip: Go early or in the evening when it is quiet – and look at the hotels around it.

Landawirsee in the Lungau💎 Hidden gem

A mountain lake at 1,968 metres, crystal clear. For the Landawirsee you need sure-footedness and good shoes. Plan the hut as a stage – and bring a towel. You will want to go in.

Lake Landawirsee lies in the Lungau at just under 1,970 metres in a cirque landscape surrounded by rock walls and alpine meadows. The water is crystal clear and cold. The ascent is not extremely long but steep and requires sure-footedness; hiking boots and weather protection are a must. Plan several hours depending on the starting point (check times beforehand).

The Landawirsee hut lies on the way and is a good destination for a rest and overnight stay (reserve beforehand). Pack a towel and a change of clothes if you want to get in the water – it is refreshing. Afternoon thunderstorms are possible in summer: start early.

Tip: Start early, check the weather forecast and reserve the hut if you want to stay overnight.

Kaiserschmarrn at Landawirseehütte

Afterwards Kaiserschmarrn and a cool regional beer, overnight stay at the hut.

Kaiserschmarrn is a classic of Alpine cuisine: a fluffy, torn pancake with raisins, icing sugar and plum compote or apple sauce. At the hut it tastes twice as good after the hike. With it there is beer or Almdudler from the region.

The hut offers simple rooms or dormitories (check prices and reservation beforehand). If you stay overnight you experience sunset and starry sky in the silence of the mountains.

Belle Époque architecture & Straubinger Platz

In the 19th century Bad Gastein was a fashionable spa resort for emperors, kings and artists. Magnificent hotels in Belle Époque style line the slope above the waterfall. Some are restored and open, others stand empty – this contrast of glamour and decay is the appeal of the place.

A walk through the village with a view of the façades, small cafés and art galleries takes two hours. At Straubinger Platz and in the lanes around the waterfall you find the most beautiful views.

Tip: Go to the waterfall in the evening and look at the lights of the hotels.

Felsentherme Bad Gastein

The Felsentherme is a modern thermal spa right in the rock, with warm, mineral-rich Gastein thermal water, pools inside and outside and a sauna area. If you want to relax after a mountain tour, this is the right place; from the outdoor pool you look onto the mountains.

Prices and opening times change (check beforehand). The water has been used for centuries; plan two to three hours and bring swimwear.

Tip: Plan the spa for the afternoon after a hike.

Stubnerkogel & suspension bridge

The Stubnerkogel cable car takes you in minutes from the village to about 2,200 metres. At the top await a suspension bridge crossing a gorge, a viewpoint overlooking the Gastein valley and hiking paths through alpine terrain. In winter it is a ski area.

The ride is worthwhile in clear weather; check operating times and weather beforehand. Bring a jacket – it is much cooler at the top.

Tip: Go up in clear weather and early in the day.

What makes Austria special

About two thirds of the country are Alps. Viennese coffee-house culture is UNESCO intangible heritage. In the 19th century Bad Gastein was the spa of emperors and kings, Empress Sisi came for a cure. Austria has been neutral since 1955. If you only have a weekend: go to the mountains. A lake at almost 2,000 metres beats any pedestrian zone.

FAQ

Is the Landawirsee for beginners?

You need sure-footedness and good shoes.

How many days do you need in Bad Gastein?
Two to three days for village, spa and a mountain tour.
Is Bad Gastein also nice in winter?
Yes, in winter there are ski areas (Stubnerkogel, Graukogel) and the waterfall turns to ice; the spas are especially cosy then.
