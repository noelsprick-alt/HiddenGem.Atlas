What makes the Ruhr special

For over 150 years the Ruhr was Germany’s industrial heart. In December 2018 the last hard-coal mine closed in Bottrop. Zeche Zollverein in Essen has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2001, in 2010 the Ruhr was European Capital of Culture. Mines became museums, spoil tips viewpoints, blast furnaces climbing parks.
